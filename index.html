What we do

Six crafts.
One line.

From the first cliché to the final firing, every step happens under one roof — so colour, register and finish stay exactly as designed.

01

Screen printing

Silk-screen printing with ceramic and UV inks — crisp multi-colour logos, fine lines and full wrap-around designs, fired for permanence.

  • Ceramic inks
  • UV inks
  • 360° wrap
  • Up to 6 colours
02

Hot stamping

Gold, silver and holographic foils pressed onto glass for a mirror-bright, luxurious accent.

03

Coating

Matte, gloss, soft-touch, frost and gradient finishes — the whole bottle becomes the colour of your brand.

04

Bottle painting

Full-body and gradient painting, plus hand-finished details for limited editions and gift series.

05

Cliché making

We produce our own clichés, stamping dies and screen stencils in-house — faster samples, exact reproduction.

06

Pad printing & decals

Tampography for curved and hard-to-reach surfaces, and ceramic decal transfer for photographic, multi-colour artwork on glass and ceramics.

  • Tampography
  • Decal transfer
  • Glass & ceramics

How it works

From sketch
to shelf.

01

Brief

Tell us about the bottle, the brand and the run. We advise on technique, inks and budget.

02

Design & 3D

We adapt artwork to the glass shape and build 3D previews — including volumetric relief decoration.

03

Packed, palletised and delivered to your bottling line — in Moldova and abroad.

Since 2000

A quarter-century
on glass.

Agro Alim Grup was founded in Chișinău in October 2000 by engineer Serghei Drăguțanu, and became the first company in Moldova to decorate glass containers for wine and the beverage industry.

Today we combine screen printing, hot stamping, coating, painting, pad printing and decal transfer to serve wineries, distilleries and beverage producers — from boutique runs to industrial volumes.

EXCLUSIV

Took part in promoting and producing EXCLUSIV vodka for the American market.

HUNTER

Design and production of the HUNTER premium vodka bottle.

ICE

3D volumetric decoration developed and modelled for ICE vodka.
